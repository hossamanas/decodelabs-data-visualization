# decodelabs-data-visualization
🚀 Milestone Achieved: Completed Project 3: Data Visualization with DecodeLabs!

Data without visualization is just numbers on a page. In this third phase of my training, I transformed clean transactional data into highly interpretable visual stories—building charts that drive decision-making.

📊 Key Visual Architecture Implemented:
• Product Revenue Mapping (Bar Chart): Visually identified Chairs ($195K+), Printers ($195K+), and Laptops ($192K+) as the dominant revenue streams, highlighting core commercial priorities.
• Proportional Integrity Scan (Pie Chart): Exposed a critical business trend showing that Cancelled and Returned categories share an almost equal slice with successful deliveries, proving operational leakage.
• Historical Trend Lines (Line Chart): Tracked monthly aggregate sales to map structural market cyclicality and capture high-frequency seasonal revenue spikes (such as May 2023 peaking at $63,836).

🛠️ Tech Stack: Python, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Automated Reporting.

The project repository is updated with the production script, high-resolution source plots, and the official visual insights deliverable report! 📈🎨
